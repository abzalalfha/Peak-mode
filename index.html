PROGRESSION

Your 90-day ramp

  • Weeks 1–2: return gradually, 3 reps in reserve, technique first.
  • Weeks 3–5: add reps, then small weight increases when every set is clean.
  • Week 6: deload; reduce sets by 40–50%.
  • Weeks 7–10: build steadily, usually 1–2 reps in reserve.
  • Week 11: consolidate and reduce fatigue.
  • Weeks 12–13: maintain strength; no forced max attempts.
  • After day 90: continue the weekly structure and schedule easier weeks every 5–8 weeks or as fatigue requires.

Start conservatively after your training break. Avoid max attempts. Stop for sharp pain and adjust volume if recovery deteriorates.
